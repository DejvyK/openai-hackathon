Three recorded backend runs with GPT-5.6 Luna and Exa produced sourced research and valid drafts. The Chrome interface and workspace adapters have local test coverage. ## Current state — judge description

Finding a useful person online is only the beginning. You still need to research their work, keep the sources, and prepare a follow-up somewhere else. AgentLayer explores a simpler handoff: let the page supply the context, then review the proposed work before saving it.

The prototype has two intended paths. A professional profile leads to a contact and follow-up proposal. A selected passage leads to a research-note proposal. The point is to offer a relevant next step beside the content, with its source still attached.

The research backend has real evidence behind it. Three recorded runs with GPT-5.6 Luna and Exa covered two public profiles and one selected passage. The resulting briefs included supported findings, and the work drafts passed validation. These runs used manually prepared contexts; they did not start in the extension. Missing contact details remained empty, and no follow-up date was invented.

Local tests cover the Chrome interface, review controls, and workspace handling, including uncertain saves. The complete live experience remains unverified: native toolbar use, real-page journeys, and saved Ambiguous contacts, linked tasks, and notes still need acceptance.

Built solo by David Král, AgentLayer aims to carry the source through every step. Its current demonstrated result is research and valid proposals, with workspace delivery still to prove.
